## Model
For my estimates of care home net profit, I used 2025 Medicaid rates Level 1 (lighter care) for about $2,540 and Level 2 (heavier care) for about $3,760 per resident per month, Oahu, SSI residents mixed with my assumed midpoint for private pay for $6000 ($4000 to $7500) residents I found on Caring Manoa website; found its capacity figures inconsistent with state data, so used it only for price ranges. My net profit figures ($35,000–$60,000) came from the Care Home Budget tab of Care_Home_vs_Realtor.xlsx build by Claude. it's expense numbers including food, substitute caregiver and etc. are estimates, not from data.

For my estimate registered nurses' $150,000 annual salary at a major hospital in Oahu is relative to Median annual wage of $136,320 ($65.54/hr) Hawaii registered nurses statewide from Hawaii’s Department of Labor publication of the BLS wage survey, May 2024.
