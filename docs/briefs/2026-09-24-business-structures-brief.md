Research Brief

Purpose
The legal structure (sole proprietorship vs. LLC) decides liability; the tax election (default vs. S-corp) decides most of the tax savings. For a one-person business, an LLC alone changes almost nothing on the tax bill; an S-corp election can cut self-employment tax once profit is steady and high enough.


Research question: Which structure lets a single owner keep more after-tax income, at what profit level does each option make sense, and what does it cost to run in Hawaii?

Is this a doable research project?  I am interested in the one person business and have friends also doing it.  I want to learn what it is that makes this decision difficult and confusing for small business owners.
