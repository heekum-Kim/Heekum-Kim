## What would change the answer
The plan: the bed counts (10 / 20 / 30) and the profit, in two or three sentences. Cite the cell for the profit figure.
The judgment call: one decision where you chose between options. The carrots-versus-mesclun cap is the natural one. Give the shadow prices, say which you’d relax first, and say what the evidence doesn’t prove. A shadow price only covers one extra bed.
What would change the answer: one or two inputs, with the direction. For example, what happens to the plan if the price or the labor rate moves, and why.
