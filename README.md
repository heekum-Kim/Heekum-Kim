# About Me

I'm Heekum Kim, a patient-focused, detail-oriented registered nurse based in Honolulu, HI, with over a decade of experience in perioperative care. Since 2012, I've worked as a Registered Nurse at Kapiolani Medical Center for Women and Children, specializing in perioperative pediatrics, adult general surgery, ENT, orthopedics, and OB-GYN care. In this role, I prepare and maintain sterile surgical fields, conduct thorough patient histories, coordinate daily operating schedules, and communicate closely with anesthesiologists and surgeons to ensure safe, well-coordinated care — from preoperative prep through the post-anesthesia care unit (PACU).

My healthcare career began in 2011 as a Nurse Aide at the same medical center, where I provided direct patient care and supported multiple units as needed. Earlier still, I served as a U.S. Army Medic at Wuerzburg Army Hospital from 2002 to 2005, delivering care in ENT and Dermatology clinics, assisting physicians in surgical procedures, and managing clinic operations during unit deployments.

I hold a Bachelor of Science in Nursing from the University of Hawaii at Manoa (2011) and am a licensed Registered Nurse (RN-70466) with current CPR BLS certification and OSHA/HIPAA training. I'm a member of the Association of periOperative Registered Nurses (AORN) and bilingual in Korean, with intermediate proficiency in Japanese. Building on this clinical foundation, I'm now pursuing an MBA at the Shidler College of Business, bringing a nurse's eye for detail and patient care into the world of business.
